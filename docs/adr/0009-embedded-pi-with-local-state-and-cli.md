# Use Pi Agent Core, SQLite, and local management commands

The first version uses a long-running TypeScript Node service embedding Pi Agent Core directly to host the unified primary thinking process. This project maintains continuous perception, scheduling, context compaction, subject state, and tools; the SnowLuma server and QQ client are external dependencies. SQLite stores events, experiences, versions of understanding, continuing state, wake-up arrangements, and action outcomes. Recall combines people, social context, full-text search, time, and importance, with a replaceable retrieval interface so semantic retrieval can be added based on recall quality. The first management interface is a local CLI supporting status, pause and resume, the life switch, memory inspection, correction and deletion, and inspection of operational logs.

Revision of 2026-10-30: spontaneous recall now chooses memories by the meaning of what is said, through an embeddings endpoint the operator configures, rather than by social context, importance, and time. Recall split its query into runs of letters, so a Chinese sentence became a few long runs that seldom appear in a memory word for word. Replayed on a snapshot over the 500 latest messages from others (474 observations and 26 recallable memories, each observation limited to memories that already existed), 2,882 of the 3,121 memories it supplied were chosen only for sharing a group with the observation, and the same top three appeared in 36% of observations. The words that did match were mostly fragments such as single digits, `on`, `an`, and `face` from face placeholders. With `qwen3-vl-embedding`, memories embedded line by line, and messages without placeholders, mentions, or the names people call the digital life, matches at 0.70 or more were related in the samples read, most from 0.65 were, and below that general lines such as headers and closing remarks crept in. At 0.65, 50 of the 365 observations with text recalled anything.

A thought therefore receives at most `embedding.limit` memories whose closest line is at least `embedding.threshold` alike, each with that line. The threshold belongs to the model and must be calibrated again for another. Memories are embedded when written and messages when they arrive, so a thought compares stored vectors and never waits on the endpoint. A message not yet embedded, or an endpoint that cannot be reached, leaves that thought without spontaneous recall rather than delaying it, and without embedding configured no memory is recalled on its own. Development and revised versions never come up this way ([ADR-0012](0012-self-maintained-development-alongside-the-operator-persona.md)). Vectors are deleted with their memory ([ADR-0003](0003-separate-runtime-control-from-social-requests.md)), and those of another model are dropped.

Deliberate recall through `recall` fuses the same vector search with its text search by reciprocal rank, so exact words such as names and QQ numbers, which embeddings seldom place first, still lead it, and text search alone answers when the endpoint cannot be reached. Its query is embedded when it is made. Because it reaches every kind and, on request, earlier versions, every memory is embedded. A deliberate query is usually a few words and scores lower than a message. Of 18 test queries on the same snapshot, short ones such as `小笼包` and `音游 大触 战绩` found their memory at 0.545 to 0.61, while the closest memory to unrelated ones such as `期末考试复习计划`, `天气预报`, and `finish_activity` stayed at 0.53 or below, so it has its own `embedding.searchThreshold`. In the two days before this change it was called four times, always with keywords separated by spaces; its three empty results came from memories not yet written and from a tool name, not from how text search splits Chinese.

Raw chat messages are not embedded: recalling one group's words into a thought elsewhere raises their sharing scope ([ADR-0001](0001-one-agent-across-group-environments.md)), and their vectors would have to expire with them ([ADR-0006](0006-experience-understanding-and-continuing-state.md)).

Revision of 2026-11-02: saved stickers also come to mind by what is said. Choosing one used to take a `fetch_custom_face_detail` call, and so a model call of its own. In the 48 hours before 2026-11-02 13:00 that call was made 28 times, 9 of them before a reply, and it usually asked for the latest 20 to 48 of 173 stickers, so older ones were rarely seen: the 49 stickers sent since 2026-10-28 were 48 different ones. Supplying the whole collection with every thought would take about 14,000 tokens of every request. Each sticker the digital life saved and described is therefore embedded by its description, again when the description changes, and its vectors go when it is deleted. The collection is known from its own save, describe, and delete actions, so a sticker saved elsewhere, such as on a phone, is not included. A description is matched by two lines: the clause from 适合, 表示, or 用于 on, which says what the sticker suits, and the whole description. Replayed on the 1,980 messages from others in those 48 hours against 172 descriptions with `qwen3-vl-embedding`, the clause found a sticker at 0.65 or more for 113 messages and the whole description for 21; matches read from 0.65 were mostly apt, and below about 0.62 they loosened. An observation whose messages are at least `embedding.stickerThreshold` alike to a description brings at most `embedding.stickerLimit` such stickers with their IDs and descriptions, each at most once a thought, and the digital life may send one with `send_custom_face` or none. They come with the observation rather than in runtime data: a sticker fits what was just said rather than standing state, and a suggestion in an observation never has to be withdrawn later. In an offline run on that data, the 172 descriptions took about 50 seconds to embed once, and 14 of the latest 300 messages brought stickers.
