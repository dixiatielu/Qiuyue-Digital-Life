# Make autonomous life configurable and distinguish actual experience from fictional daily life

To support different lifestyles for different personas, the operator can enable or disable autonomous life mode. When enabled, the digital life pursues its own life according to its persona. Permitted activities are configured separately: actual digital activities are the default, and fictional daily life may be explicitly allowed. Memory distinguishes the two types of experience; fictional narratives cannot become records of successful tool execution. This distinction allows character narratives to develop while providing a reliable basis for actual actions and their outcomes.

When autonomous life mode is disabled, full group perception, spontaneous participation, persona, and memory remain, and accepted social commitments and memory consolidation continue; only the initiation and pursuit of autonomous life are disabled. When enabled, the digital life chooses its next activity and self-directed wake-up time, while the program handles timing and enforces configurable frequency and usage limits. It may rest when nothing is pending. See [ADR-0003](0003-separate-runtime-control-from-social-requests.md) for Agent pause.
