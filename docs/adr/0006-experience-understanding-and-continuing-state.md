# Store experience records, long-term understanding, and continuing state separately

To allow the digital life both to grow from experience and reliably continue unfinished matters, store experience records, long-term understanding, and continuing state separately. Experience records retain time, people, social context, and actual outcomes; long-term understanding retains traceable and revisable knowledge, preferences, and relationship impressions; continuing state retains current activities, unfinished commitments, and self-directed wake-up arrangements. The current context selects only the material needed for the present thought. Continuing state is restored directly on restart and supplied directly during thinking; it must not depend on a chance retrieval hit.

The same Agent judges memory importance in light of its own persona, relationships, interests, and current goals. Memory consolidation is also its activity within the primary thinking process; the program handles storage and retrieval. Group experiences and memory continue to be maintained when autonomous life mode is disabled. Each thought automatically includes a small number of relevant memories to support spontaneous recall, and a tool is provided for deliberate recall.

Long-term understanding retains sources, times, and the basis for changes, distinguishing first-hand updates, others' reports, and the Agent's own inferences. A new judgment may replace an old one as the current view while preserving the history of changes. Recalling the same judgment repeatedly does not constitute new evidence. The distinction between actual experience and fictional daily life continues to follow [ADR-0005](0005-configurable-life-with-experience-provenance.md).

Trivial matters may gradually fade from everyday recall, and old details may be compressed. Processed raw events are retained for 90 days by default, with a configurable retention period; important experiences, long-term understanding, and necessary source excerpts are retained long term. Unprocessed events are not deleted because of this period. Relationship turning points, important experiences, and personal changes should have lasting influence. Unfinished commitments are managed by continuing state and do not automatically disappear through fading. See [ADR-0003](0003-separate-runtime-control-from-social-requests.md) for operator deletion of memory.
