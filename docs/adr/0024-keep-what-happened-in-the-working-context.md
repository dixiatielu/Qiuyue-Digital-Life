# Keep what happened in the working context, not what was said to no one

On 2026-11-24, the first day gemini-4-hydrogen ran the thoughts through the Interactions API ([ADR-0023](0023-load-seldom-used-tools-on-demand.md)), the digital life replied or reacted to seven message IDs that no event carried, and in one group introduced itself in answer to a question no one had asked. The instructions said that a reply without a tool call ends the thought and that to lurk is to reply empty. gemini-3.8-flash had answered such a thought with empty text. From 11:25 hydrogen wrote "No tool calls, ending thought." instead, and the working context kept that reply and gave it back with every later call, as it kept every reply. By the evening the context had held 381 of them and read as hundreds of turns of an observation answered by the same line. At 16:54 the model went on with that pattern in its own output: after the line it wrote "refresh", the role markers `thought` and `user`, and a new observation in the program's format whose message ID and text no one had sent, and then sent a sticker in answer to it. Six of the seven calls on missing IDs came from replies that repeated "…ending thought.refresh" in this way; the seventh came in the next turn of such a thought.

The same day 25 thoughts failed with the API's `failed` status. A failed thought was rolled back to where it began, and its events were observed again. Five of these had already sent nine messages, reactions, and statuses. Their messages stayed in the chats while the decisions to send them left the working context, so the digital life saw replies to what it had said without having said it.

The working context now keeps of each reply only the actions it took. Once a newer observation has entered, the replies before it keep only their calls from the next request on, and a reply that called no tool leaves no turn at all; when the thought ends, all its replies do. Observations, calls, and results stay. What a reply says reaches no one: actions go out through tools ([ADR-0010](0010-expose-native-snowluma-actions.md)), and the instructions say so. The replies after the newest observation stay as they were said, which the Interactions API requires within a turn. A reply that took no action ended its request and was never the input to one, so leaving it out costs no cache. The text and thoughts of replies that acted leave where a thought's chat records and images already do, inside the thought, so the cached prefix before it stays valid. Their originals stay archived. Thoughts were already left out of the saved context; they now leave the running one too, so a running context and a restored one are the same. A preempted thought folds its replies as well, while it still leaves its records and images for the next thought ([ADR-0002](0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md)).

The instructions now say that what a reply says reaches no one and is gone when the thought ends, so a reply that takes no action may be empty or a single word. They change, so a deploy starts a new working context, which also leaves behind the one that taught the habit.

A thought that fails after a turn has completed now keeps everything up to the end of that turn, as a cancelled one does ([ADR-0002](0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md)): its actions and their results stay in view, and the observations it decided on count as perceived. Observations that entered after that turn reached only the failed call, so they return to pending and come again. A thought with no completed turn still rolls back whole, and an operator's stop or a timeout still discards the thought. This narrows the rollback that [ADR-0017](0017-tell-the-working-context-each-thing-once.md) names among the ways an action can be missing from the context.

Before this was built, two of the day's requests were rebuilt from the archive and replayed against gemini-4-hydrogen: the one at 16:54 that wrote the made-up observation, and the one at 18:08 that preceded the self-introduction. A rebuilt request lacks the thoughts the running process held, about 7,600 of 156,000 prompt tokens at 16:54. As they had been, the 16:54 request answered with the filler line in all five replays, and the 18:08 one repeated "…ending thought.refresh" to the 32,768-token output limit in all four, each time word for word. With the replies of earlier thoughts folded, the 18:08 request still looped once in five, and with the new instructions it copied, five times in five, the filler of the four replies its own thought had made before the newest observation. With those folded too, it neither looped nor wrote filler in five replays, and four of the five reacted to or answered a message the chat held. No replay wrote an observation or cited a message ID absent from its request. The `failed` status, which arrives with no output, no steps, and no error, came in 7 of 29 replays with folded replies and in none of the nine unfolded ones; its cause is not known, and a thought it fails is handled as above.

Sending the model's text as the message, as Letta's current agent and OpenClaw's default reply mode do, was considered and set aside. There a session is one conversation and a reply has one addressee; here one attention spans every chat ([ADR-0001](0001-one-agent-across-group-environments.md), [ADR-0002](0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md)), so text would need a target, and replies, mentions, several messages, and stickers a markup the model writes by hand. The action ledger and outcome rules ([ADR-0008](0008-preserve-unknown-action-outcomes.md)), preemption between sends, the end of a thought after social actions, and permissions all rest on actions being tool calls. Nor would it have prevented this: sent as text, the 16:54 reply would have posted the made-up observation to the group. OpenClaw's mode for always-on group rooms and MaiBot's planner likewise act through tools and keep the model's text private. Also set aside: recognizing a made-up observation in a reply and discarding the calls after it, which would catch only those written where the program can read them.

Revision of 2026-11-25: with silent replies folded away, the working context retained only turns that called tools, so an occasional redundant `set_activity` call survived and snowballed into 50+ repeated calls within hours (often 2–6 identical calls inside one thought). Unchanged state updates (`set_activity`, `update_pursuit`, `set_diy_online_status`, `set_self_longnick`) now return `unchanged: true` with `details.noop = true` without mutating state or dispatching external actions, identical repeated calls within the same thought and observation window are blocked with `terminate: true`, and `foldNoopCalls` drops both no-op/blocked calls and their paired results when the thought ends (or when a newer observation enters) so they never enter the cached prefix as few-shot examples.

Because reply text is now folded out of working context rather than replayed into later thoughts, the system instructions no longer restrict ordinary reply text (forbidding chain of thought or bracketed stage directions in replies, nudging silent replies to be empty or a single word, or forbidding narrating what was seen when lurking); they keep only the rule against stage directions or action narration in sent messages, the factual note that reply text reaches no one and leaves when the thought ends, and the general rule not to call any tool when there is nothing to do or silence is chosen (removing the narrow `set_activity`-only wording).

Later on 2026-11-25: after fresh working-context resets during quiet periods, folding all silent turns left the context with only consecutive `user` observations and zero `assistant` turns, pushing `gemini-4-hydrogen` to probe tools (`read_event`, and then `finish_pursuit({"id":"none","status":"done"})` after silent local reads were folded). Rather than folding pure local read-only calls—which was reverted so legitimate lookups stay available across thoughts—a fresh main working context now seeds up to six of her own recent successful, unredacted, currently-declared chat actions interleaved with their preceding processed chat events as alternating `user` / `assistant(toolCall)` / `toolResult` turns (reusing authentic same-model `thoughtSignature`s from recent `context_records` when available), so new contexts begin with normal multi-turn alternation without leaking forgotten or undeclared history.
