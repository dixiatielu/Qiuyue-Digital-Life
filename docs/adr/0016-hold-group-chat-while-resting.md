# Hold group chat that is not addressed to the digital life while it rests

[ADR-0015](0015-offer-free-time-when-chats-go-quiet-and-let-rest-be-chosen.md) let the digital life mark a rest until an activity it will wake for, and left open whether messages arriving during a rest should be treated differently. From 2026-10-30 12:00 to 2026-11-02 12:00 it rested three nights from about 22:30 to 09:00 and took one nap. Over those rests it still made 942 model calls, about 124 million prompt tokens, a quarter of all it used in that time. 695 of those calls ended without any action, most often with a line such as "继续安静睡觉，不参与群聊。" The busiest group sent 924, 78, and 568 messages over the three nights, and only 4 of them mentioned or replied to it.

While the digital life rests, messages and notices in a group that are not addressed to it no longer start or steer a thought. Addressed has the meaning of [ADR-0002](0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md): a message that mentions it or replies to one it sent. A notice is addressed to it when it is the target, as of a poke, or when someone else did something to it, such as banning it or inviting it into a group. Whether an event is addressed is settled when it arrives. Private chats, addressed group events, its own planned activities and commitments, and the program's own events reach it as before. During a rest an ambient event no longer starts a thought on its own after `limits.ambientDelayMs`; it rides along with the next event that does.

A rest lasts while a wake marked as rest is pending and not yet due, and only while autonomous life mode is on, since rest belongs to it. It ends when that wake comes due, when the digital life finishes or cancels it, or when life mode is switched off. The program records when a rest began, so a restart does not lose track of one.

Whenever an event starts or steers a thought during rest, or the rest ends, everything still held comes at once, as one `rest_backlog` note. The held events are recorded as perceived, and the note's observation carries each group's chat from its oldest still-held event, in the line-per-item form of `read_chat_record`: time, reference, speaker, and text, with reactions under the message they react to. Observed as events, the same chat would come in batches of `limits.batchSize`, 924 messages making about 47 steered turns, and as event objects it would be about six times the size: the busiest of the three nights was about 132,000 tokens as event objects and about 23,000 as a record. The records of one note together take at most a quarter of `limits.contextTokens`, shared among the groups by how much each held. Beyond that a record leaves out its earliest lines and says from when to read them with `read_chat_record`. The note accompanies the waking event in the same observation even beyond `limits.batchSize`, including when a life wake starts its own thought ([ADR-0015](0015-offer-free-time-when-chats-go-quiet-and-let-rest-be-chosen.md)). A failed thought leaves the note pending for retry or restart along with the waking event.

What a rest held does not count toward the next memory review, which is triggered by events observed one by one. The review still reaches those messages through the chat record, which it reads back up to a day.

Dropping held messages would lose experiences the digital life perceives ([ADR-0011](0011-perceive-all-snowluma-push-events.md)); here they stay recorded and are read on waking. A cheaper model that decides whether to wake the main one was not chosen, because the digital life's single attention and identity would then depend on a second judge ([ADR-0002](0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md)).

Being woken for a thought does not itself end rest: the digital life can respond and go back to sleep, and later group chat waits for the next waking event or the end of rest. Each note covers only the new backlog. Getting up early still means finishing or rescheduling the activity it was resting until.

This narrows ADR-0002's statement that all environment events enter one perception pipeline and may be combined into observations: the events a rest held are combined into one note and read as a record. Rest still never cancels a thought already under way.

Revision of 2026-11-02: an early waking event now brings the backlog into that thought. Previously the note waited for rest to end, so a mention or private message woke the digital life without the accumulated context, leaving events unobserved even after it thought. The batching limit must not postpone that context to another thought. The system instructions are unchanged to preserve the existing working context; the note itself says what waited and carries its record.
