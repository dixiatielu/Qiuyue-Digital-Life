# Offer free time when chats go quiet, and let the digital life choose when it rests

From 2026-10-29 04:23 to 2026-10-30 19:48 the digital life had about 1,010 thoughts started by chats, 32 memory reviews, and 13 life wakes. It did browse Qzone at about half of the life wakes, but it never searched or read anything online of its own accord. Its own life plans fell on the hour or half hour, which were mostly the middle of a conversation, and 4 of the 13 life wakes were steered into a chat thought already under way. Meanwhile there were about fifteen daytime stretches of 15 to 46 minutes in which nobody else wrote anything, and none of them held a life wake. Memory review, by contrast, waits for a quiet moment, so the free time went to review. A trigger counted in events, like review's, would have the opposite effect: a busy group reaches the count in minutes and a quiet afternoon never does.

The program therefore offers a free-time wake: a life wake that comes when the chats have been quiet for a stretch, no thought is running, and nothing is waiting. The length of the stretch is drawn at random within configured bounds each time it starts, so the wake does not come at a fixed frequency. A free-time wake keeps a configured gap from every other life wake, before and after, and stays within a daily limit. It says only that the chats have been quiet and for how long; what it shows of the digital life's days is what any life wake shows ([ADR-0014](0014-pursuits-and-a-life-wake-that-sees-its-own-days.md)). It carries no count of what may be waiting on any platform, because each online activity is offered as one line of equal weight, and a count for one platform would favour it over reading the news or any platform added later. The digital life may use the time or rest.

This departs from [ADR-0005](0005-configurable-life-with-experience-provenance.md), under which the digital life chooses its own wake-up times and the program handles only timing and limits. A free-time wake is a wake the digital life did not arrange. Like memory maintenance, it starts a thought without deciding anything in it, and the self-directed wake-ups the digital life schedules remain its own.

When to rest is the digital life's to choose, not a fixed period set by the program. When it schedules an activity it will wake for, such as getting up in the morning or the end of a nap, it can mark that it is resting until then, and no free-time wake comes before that activity comes due or is cancelled. Rest suits a persona whose hours are irregular and changes from night to night. If the digital life forgets to mark rest, the cost is a free-time wake at most once per gap, at which it can still mark rest. Rest holds back only free-time wakes. Its own planned activities, commitments, and messages still come. Whether messages that arrive while it rests should be treated differently is a separate decision.

A life wake, whether scheduled or offered, is no longer steered into a thought already under way, and it is not claimed together with chat events. It starts a thought of its own, which new messages may steer as usual.

Two facts that the digital life would otherwise have to infer are now stated. Instructions say that what it sees online comes back to mind later only if it saves it with remember. A life wake says when no pursuits are under way.
