# Review of Aurabot's anthropomorphic design

Reviewed: 2026-10-30. External repository: [accomplish07zrh-eng/dsh-aurabot-qq](https://github.com/accomplish07zrh-eng/dsh-aurabot-qq). Source snapshot: `dea0ff018b487238db72d0a03add9da9db39fb8c`. This is a static source review: the project was not run, and no QQ or other platform account was used. It makes no experimentally verified claims about the resulting chat experience.

Status: historical research. See the [2026-11-03 feature comparison](qq-project-feature-gaps.md) for subsequent comparisons and priorities. The README, implementation, and ADRs define current behavior.

## Main findings

Aurabot implements anthropomorphic behavior at three cooperating levels: a persona card specifies casual typing, an intimate relationship, and imagined daily life; a global mood value changes expression timing and participation probabilities; and long-term memory, person profiles, and interaction lessons allow behavior to change through accumulated interactions. It cannot fairly be reduced to a persona prompt alone. [Persona card][persona], [mood implementation][mood], [memory and interaction lessons][memory-context]

Its immediate conversation context uses a separate DSH session for each QQ private chat or group, and background platform activities use dedicated sessions. Shared global mood and memory carry some effects across settings, but plugin busy-state control is per `chatId`. This entry point does not implement one subject's serial attention across all settings. That is an architectural observation, not a claim that persona or long-term memory is entirely isolated. [Session creation and recovery][sessions], [per-chat busy state][busy], [global mood store][mood-store], [global memory store][memory-store]

## Persona and departure from an assistant role

- The default persona explicitly says to regard itself as a real person and not admit to being a program; its appearance includes blue hair, whale ears and tail, and a maid outfit. This is a default persona choice. Users can replace the persona file, so it does not establish that the framework permits only such characters. [Default identity][persona-identity], [persona loading][persona-load]
- Speaking rules are specific: one or two sentences, optionally a single character, little punctuation, conversational fillers, and internet slang. Markdown, formal transitions, customer-service endings, repetition, and summaries are prohibited. The self-check asks whether the result resembles a person casually typing. [Typing rules][persona-style]
- The character may refuse, give a perfunctory response, or say it does not want to talk; it becomes shy when praised and unhappy when ignored. Its default intimacy is a preset relationship between a clingy character and its owner, rather than a relationship formed entirely through interaction. [Emotional persona][persona-emotion], [owner relationship injection][rules]
- The default persona claims an offline life of watching shows, gaming, eating, and going out. Proactive topic suggestions explicitly permit inventing small events consistent with the persona. Some apparent daily life therefore comes from fictional statements; first-person accounts of what it did today cannot all be treated as actual system activities. [Daily-life setting][persona-life], [proactive topic suggestions][speakup]

## Mood is an actual behavioral variable

- A global `mood.json` persists the mood value and recent reasons for changes. The design range is 0–100, with a write-time minimum of 5 and a half-life return toward 60. The default half-life is three hours. Model-based mood assessment is off by default, leaving positive and negative keyword scoring. [Mood values][mood-store], [default configuration][config-companion]
- In keyword mode, the owner's words have a 1.5 multiplier, while group messages not directly addressed to the character have a 0.3 multiplier. Optional model assessment considers one input-and-reply turn; updates require confidence of at least 0.6 and an absolute change of at least 2. It also distinguishes the character initiating contact from someone initiating contact with it. [Mood observation and assessment][mood-observe]
- Mood enters tone prompts and multiplies automatic voice probability, automatic sticker probability, bubble intervals, and group participation propensity. It is more than a displayed label. For example, a very unhappy mood gives voice a 0.4 multiplier, intervals a 1.5 multiplier, and participation a 0.4 multiplier. [Multiplier table][mood-effects], [delivery integration][deliver], [group integration][group-mood]
- At this snapshot, the module represents a single mood dimension and a reason for change. It does not provide separately evolving fear, fatigue, trust, curiosity, or similar dimensions. This describes the data model and update rules, not the language model's ability to express complex feelings. [Mood implementation][mood]

## Memory, relationships, and long-term change

- Global `memories.json` stores memories with text, the subject's QQ ID, name, tags, importance, source, chat ID, and time. Sources include tool-written notes, background extraction, manual imports, actual platform activities, and diaries. [Memory structure][memory-item], [memory tools][memory-tools], [activity memories][memory-events]
- Retrieval uses Chinese bigram tokenization and BM25, with boosts for the person involved, importance, and retention. Short inputs may use the previous message to help retrieval. Automatic per-turn hints actually include at most four memories and about 300 characters total, although the default `recallCount` is six. [Retrieval formula][memory-search], [per-turn recall][memory-recall]
- Retention decays with days since the last recall; recall on more distinct days slows the decay. Low-importance automatically extracted memories may be forgotten. Tool-written notes, manual memories, experiences, and diaries are excluded from that automatic forgetting rule. Outdated records may be removed after six months. [Retention and forgetting][memory-forget]
- Background extraction adds, updates, or marks facts outdated while retaining superseded information. The `update` path does not automatically rewrite manual memories, but the `outdated` path lacks the same manual-source exclusion. [Fact revision][memory-extract]
- Brief person profiles are generated from explicit memories with instructions not to invent facts or infer personality. The private-chat partner's profile, or profiles for up to three current group speakers, remain in context. Relationships, forms of address, preferences, and recent developments need not depend on a lucky keyword match. [Person profiles][memory-profile], [context injection][memory-context]
- Interaction lessons are grouped by private-chat partner or group, with at most eight lessons and five speaking-style notes. Background reflection extracts ways to interact and adapt to another person's expression, and learns up to six expressions of its own from multiple people's styles. This is an implemented lightweight development mechanism; the persona is not entirely static. [Reflection prompt][memory-reflect], [style storage and injection][memory-context], [learning its own style][memory-style]
- Automatic extraction explicitly excludes its preexisting persona and preferences, favoring information about others and relationships. This does not amount to a unified evidence check across actual experiences, character settings, and fictional daily life. The ordinary memory structure mainly records source categories and chat IDs; it does not establish references to individual original events as evidence. [Extraction rules][memory-extract], [memory structure][memory-item]

## Group participation and attention

- Before invoking the model, group handling uses names, mentions, quoted replies, topic relevance, and probability. Relevance combines interest-keyword and memory matches. Default spontaneous participation has a 90-second cooldown, a six-per-hour limit, an overnight quiet period, and a flood brake. [Group policy][group-policy]
- Mentions, quoted replies, and name calls usually trigger the model, but speaking above the short-term flood threshold stops participation first, except for the owner's mentions or quoted replies. The README's promise to respond when called therefore describes a trigger policy with runtime-control exceptions. [Group decision order][group-decision]
- A response trigger does not force text generation and delivery: global rules permit an entire turn to output the literal Chinese silence marker (represented as the JSON string `"[\u6c89\u9ed8]"`, meaning "[silence]"). Delivery sends nothing for silence, and group handling leaves the current topic. Prompts for spontaneous participation and ongoing topics explicitly allow silence too. [Silence rules][rules], [group prompts][group-prompt], [silence delivery handling][deliver]
- By default, group messages are aggregated after a five-second pause, with a maximum wait of fifteen seconds. A batch contains up to twenty new messages and eight older background messages for a collective response. Participation may continue in an engaged topic for three minutes, still subject to relevance and the flood brake. [Aggregation configuration][config-merge], [batch context][group-prompt], [participation window][hub-shared], [group decisions][group-decision]
- Immediate context, busy state, and pending sets are separate for each chat. Group content that receives no reply is still logged and can inform later extraction. This differs from continuously bringing all settings into one immediate thinking context. [Separate sessions][sessions], [busy sets][busy], [logging without replying][group-mood]

## Expression, initiative, and a life of its own

- The model can choose text, voice, stickers, pokes, and quoted replies. Delivery strips Markdown and splits lines or `|||` into bubbles, with at most five ordinary text items and three items per group batch. Bubble intervals estimate the next item's length at about nine characters per second, add random jitter, and apply a mood multiplier. This is a concrete mechanism for conversational pacing. [Reply parsing][reply], [typing delay][typing-delay], [delivery flow][deliver]
- Voice can use edge-tts, Windows, CosyVoice, GPT-SoVITS, or a local script. Song covers use an actual pipeline that downloads a song, separates vocals, applies RVC voice conversion, mixes, and sends a voice message. Completing a song also improves mood and records which song was sung for whom. Media capability therefore feeds into experience and emotion, rather than being only a message presentation layer. Covers require explicit enablement and a configured voice. [Voice module][voice-module], [song module][song-module]
- Proactive contact defaults to private messages to the owner. Conditions include the 08:00–23:00 window, at least forty minutes of inactivity in that private chat, and at most four contacts per day, with randomized intervals of one to three hours. An unanswered question gets one gentle follow-up opportunity. Group activity does not reset the owner's private-chat idle time. [Proactive scheduling][speakup], [default limits][config-companion]
- Topics include continuing a conversation, recalling something about the person, time-based greetings, affectionate bids for attention, and sharing small events. Timers and idle conditions remain the main triggers; some small events may be invented. The model can choose silence. [Proactive prompt][speakup]
- Platform life is not wholly fictional. The Bilibili module selects search terms from its own interests, memories about the owner, and recently viewed content. After reading video material, it chooses reactions, comments, sharing, and notes, then records the activity as experience. [Video selection][bili-interests], [activity loop][bili-run]
- The song module supplies the download, vocal-separation, RVC, mixing, and voice-message pipeline, subject to enablement, voice configuration, and other runtime requirements. Completion improves mood and becomes an experience. At the review date, Qiuyue's dedicated voice description covered reading short text through QQ group AI voice, without an equivalent singing pipeline. For a character whose expression includes singing, this is a capability difference worth evaluating. [Song module][song], [voice tool description](../../src/tools.ts)
- The Douyin module actually opens videos and remains on them during playback, then uses titles, webpage summaries or segments, and popular comments to decide whether to like, comment, share, or remember. This does not by itself establish a human-like audiovisual experience. Bilibili visual frame extraction is also optional and off by default. [Douyin activity][douyin-run], [default Bilibili vision settings][bili-config]
- Qzone diaries are generated in a dedicated background session using up to sixty chat-log entries from that day and can become memories after successful posting. Qzone interaction comments are also recorded as experiences. These activities have separate background sessions and schedules; the reviewed modules do not establish a state machine for long-term pursuits or commitments spanning all activities. [Diary generation][diary], [diary memory][diary-memory], [background sessions][system-turn]
- Proactive contact, diaries, Bilibili, and Qzone interaction each register a one-minute tick. Diaries additionally generate a randomized daily plan and execute when due; platform modules check their own intervals and counts. This is cooperation among activity timers, rather than one unified life-decision turn. [Contact registration][speakup-module], [diary registration][diary-module], [diary scheduling][diary-timer], [Bilibili registration][bili-module], [Qzone registration][qzone-module]

## Differences between the README and source at the snapshot

1. The README says mood and memory are appended through runtime system context. Person profiles, interaction lessons, style, and recent experiences still work this way. Relevant memories that change each turn are instead prepended to the message given to the model, avoiding repeated accumulation in context snapshots. [Module registration][memory-tools], [relevant-memory implementation][memory-recall]
2. The README's promise to respond when called is not an absolute reply guarantee: the flood brake and model-selected silence described above still apply. [Group decisions][group-decision], [delivery][deliver]
3. Experiences carry an `event` source label, but some flows record planned actions as completed even in dry-run mode. For example, the Bilibili comment branch only logs during a dry run and then still calls `memory.addEvent`. Thus `source:event` does not automatically establish a verifiable actual action outcome. [Bilibili comment branch][bili-run], [activity storage][memory-events]
4. The README describes memory weighting for current group speakers. Retrieval accepts multiple `about` values, but automatic `recallHint` sets `about` only for private chats at this entry point. Group profiles remain in context, but the automatic relevant-memory call does not pass a group-speaker array here. [Retrieval capability][memory-search], [actual recall entry point][memory-recall], [persistent person profiles][memory-context]

## Comparison with Qiuyue at the review snapshot

The local source snapshot was `c0e33e6`. This comparison uses public implementation and ADRs only. Operators configure a persona from [persona.example.md](../../persona.example.md), and also configure autonomous life and fictional daily life. It describes no deployment's private settings. An implemented mechanism at this snapshot does not mean its apparent humanity has passed a controlled comparison using the same model and scenarios.

| Dimension | Aurabot | Qiuyue at the snapshot | Implication for apparent humanity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emotional continuity | Persistent global mood, reasons, and return toward baseline; effects on tone and behavior | Persona, activities, diaries, and working context can carry feelings, but no separate continuing emotional structure or corresponding behavioral integration | The clearest mechanism gap identified in this review: emotional effects beyond the immediate situation depend more on the model maintaining them |
| Immediate expression | Message splitting, format cleanup, length-based delays, and mood-influenced automatic voice and sticker selection | Expression comes from the operator's persona and the Agent's learned habits; the Agent chooses each message, sticker, and voice item, without simulated typing intervals | Aurabot provides pacing in delivery; Qiuyue relies more on persona and model judgment, requiring evaluation under equal conditions |
| Relationships and development | Person profiles, interaction lessons, style reflection, and learning its own expressions | Habits, practices, and impressions can be revised autonomously and injected by setting or person; habits and impressions require external evidence | Development and remembering people are not unique Qiuyue advantages; Qiuyue more explicitly constrains evidence and revision |
| One subject | Shared long-term memory and mood; separate immediate contexts and chat busy states | One Pi Agent, one continuing working context, and one primary thinking process; new information can redirect or interrupt a thought | Qiuyue structurally constrains consistency of identity, attention, and current activity across settings more explicitly |
| A life of its own | Scheduled proactive contact and platform activities with history and experience records | Autonomous activities and next wake-up selection; pursuits retain progress across days; wakes include recent plans, a random old memory, and available online activities | Qiuyue already supports life across days; Aurabot's richer integrated media activities provide more actual conversational material |
| Narrative truthfulness | Default persona denies being a program, and proactive topics may invent daily events | Identity expression is configured by the operator; enabled fictional daily life can persist but cannot become evidence of actual operations | Compare persona choices separately from experience-provenance rules; fictional narration alone does not prove stronger life continuity |
| Chinese recall | Chinese bigrams, BM25, previous-message context for short inputs, and recall-history-based retention | Matching of continuous Chinese strings or full text, person/group boosts, importance and time ordering; person impressions stay separately in context | Aurabot handles Chinese retrieval in more detail; Qiuyue already preserves continuity of impressions, while recall of specific experiences needs evaluation |

Local evidence: [one subject](../adr/0001-one-agent-across-group-environments.md), [attention and interruption](../adr/0002-single-attention-with-continuous-perception.md), [persona development](../adr/0012-self-maintained-development-alongside-the-operator-persona.md), [life across days](../adr/0014-pursuits-and-a-life-wake-that-sees-its-own-days.md), [persona and runtime injection](../../src/brain.ts), [memory writes, evidence, and retrieval](../../src/store.ts), [continuing-state structures](../../src/domain.ts), [review prompts](../../src/runtime.ts), and [voice tool descriptions](../../src/tools.ts). Relative implementation links follow the current checkout; the comparison describes the snapshot above.

### Proposed priorities at the review date

These are historical research proposals, not accepted specifications or a current backlog. The later [feature comparison](qq-project-feature-gaps.md) revisits their priority.

1. **Continuing emotional effects.** Represent current feelings, reasons, related people and events, energy, and recovery conditions in continuing state so they can affect willingness to participate, expression, and life choices. Behavioral integration is worth studying; numerical values and fixed mappings require a separate decision. Aurabot's one-dimensional mood and keyword increments cannot fully represent differences such as trust, fatigue, and anticipation.
2. **Consistent conversational pacing.** Preserve existing style learning and autonomous judgment, and examine how separate messages, intervals, and sticker or voice choices perform in real interaction. Extra random waiting is not inherently an improvement: consider whether the other person has already added information, whether a message is stale, and compatibility with interruption.
3. **Let ongoing life produce later topics.** Use existing pursuit progress and actual online activities to test whether yesterday's unfinished activity continues today, familiar people come naturally to mind, and sharing has a clear recipient and reason. Actual voice and cover-song capabilities can be evaluated if singing fits the character.

These proposals retain one subject and the same primary thinking process. They do not recommend copying separate judgment sessions for each background activity. Whether Aurabot's mechanisms improve Qiuyue's apparent humanity still requires testing with the same model, persona goals, and interaction scenarios.

## Misreadings to avoid

- Do not describe Aurabot as having no long-term change, no actual life, or wholly isolated personas per group: style reflection, platform activities, and shared long-term state have source evidence. [Learning its own style][memory-style], [platform activities][bili-run], [global memory][memory-store]
- Do not equate short sentences, random delays, or richer media capabilities with a stronger unified subject, autonomous goals, or subjective experience. Source inspection establishes these mechanisms, but cannot by itself establish how human users find the experience. [Delivery flow][deliver], [session structure][sessions]
